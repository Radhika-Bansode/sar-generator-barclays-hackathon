MONEY LAUNDERING TYPOLOGIES – BANKING / SAR CONTEXT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

TYPOLOGY 1: STRUCTURING / SMURFING

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition:
Deliberate splitting of large transactions into smaller amounts to evade regulatory reporting thresholds and monitoring systems.

Regulatory Context:

FinCEN: Structuring to evade CTR requirements ($10,000 threshold)
UK NCA: Indicators of deliberate reporting avoidance behavior

Red Flag Indicators:
✓ Multiple transactions just below threshold
✓ Repeated deposits of similar values (patterned amounts)
✓ High-frequency small transactions within short time window
✓ Transactions spread across channels (cash, online, branch)
✓ Linked accounts used for fragmentation

Example Pattern:
Customer performs:

₹9,80,000
₹9,75,000
₹9,90,000
over 7–10 days
Total exceeds reporting threshold but individual transactions avoid detection

Suspicion Insight:
Indicates intentional evasion of regulatory reporting, not normal financial activity

TYPOLOGY 2: RAPID FLOW-THROUGH / LAYERING

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition:
Movement of funds through an account rapidly to obscure origin and create audit trail complexity.

Characteristics:

Very short time between credit and debit (24–72 hours)
Minimal balance retention
Continuous inflow-outflow cycles

Red Flag Indicators:
✓ Funds credited and quickly transferred out
✓ Multiple outgoing beneficiaries
✓ High transaction velocity
✓ No accumulation of funds
✓ Sudden spike in activity

Example Pattern:
Day 1–3: ₹50 lakhs received
Day 2–4: ₹48 lakhs transferred out
Retention: <5%

Suspicion Insight:
Consistent with layering stage of money laundering, aimed at obscuring fund origin

TYPOLOGY 3: MULE / COLLECTION ACCOUNT

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition:
Account used to collect funds from multiple unrelated parties and redistribute them.

Red Flag Indicators:
✓ 20+ unique incoming counterparties
✓ No apparent relationship between senders
✓ Incoming funds from multiple banks/countries
✓ Immediate consolidation and outward transfer
✓ Lack of business justification

Example Pattern:

40+ credits from different individuals
Total ₹60 lakhs in 5 days
Funds transferred to 3 accounts within 48 hours

Suspicion Insight:
Indicates mule account or intermediary laundering node, often linked to organized crime

TYPOLOGY 4: GEOGRAPHIC DISPERSION ANOMALY

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition:
Transactions originating from multiple geographically distant or unrelated locations.

Red Flag Indicators:
✓ Transactions from multiple cities/countries in short timeframe
✓ No travel or business linkage
✓ Rapid switching between locations
✓ Multiple foreign counterparties

Example Pattern:

Transactions from India, UAE, UK within 48 hours
No declared international activity

Suspicion Insight:
Indicates distributed control of account or network-based laundering activity

TYPOLOGY 5: ACCOUNT ACTIVITY SPIKE (DORMANCY BREAK)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition:
Sudden surge in activity after a period of low or no account usage.

Red Flag Indicators:
✓ Dormant account becomes highly active
✓ Large inflows followed by rapid outflows
✓ Activity inconsistent with historical behavior

Example Pattern:

No activity for 6 months
Suddenly ₹80 lakhs transacted in 1 week

Suspicion Insight:
Suggests account activation for laundering cycle or mule usage

TYPOLOGY 6: RAPID FLOW NETWORK / MULTI-COUNTERPARTY TRANSFER

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition:
Funds routed through multiple counterparties to create complexity and obscure audit trail.

Red Flag Indicators:
✓ Funds split across multiple beneficiaries
✓ Complex routing patterns
✓ Repeated inflow-outflow cycles
✓ Multiple linked accounts involved

Example Pattern:

₹1 crore received
Split into 10 transfers of ₹10 lakhs each to different beneficiaries

Suspicion Insight:
Indicates structured layering using network-based fund dispersion

TYPOLOGY 7: CROSS-BORDER HIGH-RISK TRANSFERS

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition:
Movement of funds across jurisdictions, particularly high-risk or sanctioned countries.

Red Flag Indicators:
✓ Transfers to/from high-risk jurisdictions
✓ Repeated international transfers with no business purpose
✓ Routing through multiple countries
✓ Use of foreign banks with no clear link

Example Pattern:

Funds sent to high-risk country within hours of receipt
No documented trade or remittance purpose

Suspicion Insight:
Indicates potential terrorist financing, sanctions evasion, or international laundering

TYPOLOGY 8: CASH-INTENSIVE ACTIVITY

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition:
High volume of cash transactions inconsistent with customer profile or business type.

Red Flag Indicators:
✓ Frequent large cash deposits
✓ Cash usage exceeds expected behavior
✓ Lack of supporting invoices or business activity
✓ Structured cash deposits

Example Pattern:

Monthly cash deposits ₹20 lakhs
Declared income ₹3 lakhs/month

Suspicion Insight:
Cash usage suggests placement stage of illicit funds due to low traceability

TYPOLOGY 9: INCOME / PROFILE MISMATCH

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition:
Transaction activity inconsistent with declared KYC profile (income, occupation).

Red Flag Indicators:
✓ Transactions exceed declared income capacity
✓ Occupation does not justify transaction type
✓ Sudden unexplained wealth
✓ High-value international transfers

Example Pattern:

Student account receives ₹40 lakhs in 10 days
No known income source

Suspicion Insight:
Indicates account misuse, mule involvement, or undisclosed income source

TYPOLOGY 10: ROUND NUMBER / ARTIFICIAL PATTERNING

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition:
Transactions executed in exact, repetitive values indicating non-natural behavior.

Red Flag Indicators:
✓ Repeated round values (₹50,000 / ₹1,00,000)
✓ Lack of variability in transaction size
✓ Occurs alongside structuring or layering

Example Pattern:

25 transactions of exactly ₹1,00,000

Suspicion Insight:
Suggests engineered or automated transaction behavior, not organic usage

TYPOLOGY 11: KYC MANIPULATION / PROFILE TAMPERING

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition:
Frequent or suspicious updates to customer profile to evade detection.

Red Flag Indicators:
✓ Multiple KYC changes in short period
✓ Income/occupation updates before suspicious activity
✓ Unverified profile changes
✓ Address changes across regions

Example Pattern:

Income updated from ₹3L → ₹25L
Suspicious transactions begin within 5 days

Suspicion Insight:
Indicates intentional manipulation of risk profile to bypass AML controls

CROSS-TYPOLOGY RISK AMPLIFIERS

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

High-risk classification increases when multiple typologies occur together:

✓ Structuring + Layering
✓ Mule account + Cross-border transfers
✓ Activity spike + Income mismatch
✓ KYC manipulation + rapid transactions

Interpretation:
Multiple typologies indicate coordinated laundering behavior rather than isolated anomaly

REGULATORY ALIGNMENT

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

FinCEN Requirements:

Clear articulation of why activity is suspicious
Link between transactions and typologies
Evidence-backed reasoning

UK NCA Expectations:

Suspicion must be based on reasonable grounds
Narrative must connect behavior → typology → risk
No assumption of crime, but strong suspicion logic
