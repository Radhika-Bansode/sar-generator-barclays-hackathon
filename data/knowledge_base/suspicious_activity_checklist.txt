SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY CHECKLIST
Quick Reference Guide for AML Compliance Officers
Financial Intelligence Unit - India (FIU-IND)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ACCOUNT OPENING & CUSTOMER BEHAVIOR RED FLAGS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

IDENTIFICATION & DOCUMENTATION
□ Customer reluctant to provide complete identification documents
□ Identification documents appear altered, forged, or counterfeit
□ Customer provides minimal or obviously false contact information
□ Multiple customers provide same address or contact details
□ Customer uses multiple names or spelling variations
□ Address is mail drop, virtual office, or vacant property
□ Customer appears to be nominee for undisclosed beneficial owner
□ Power of attorney used to obscure true account control
□ Customer shows no knowledge of their stated business activities

BEHAVIORAL INDICATORS
□ Customer is secretive or evasive about transaction purpose
□ Customer unusually anxious or nervous during interactions
□ Customer makes unusual inquiries about CTR/STR reporting thresholds
□ Customer attempts to convince staff not to file reports
□ Customer explicitly requests transactions avoid reporting
□ Customer shows sophisticated knowledge of AML regulations
□ Customer threatens staff when asked routine KYC questions
□ Customer changes transaction when learning of reporting requirement
□ Customer provides contradictory information across visits
□ Customer accompanied by person who dominates conversation

ACCOUNT ACTIVITY PATTERNS
□ Customer maintains multiple accounts with no apparent purpose
□ Accounts opened in quick succession (within days/weeks)
□ Similar transaction patterns across multiple accounts
□ Accounts dormant then suddenly highly active
□ Customer conducts business unusual for stated occupation
□ Account activity inconsistent with customer's age or profile
□ Customer refuses to maintain minimum balances to avoid scrutiny
□ Frequent address or contact information changes

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
CASH TRANSACTION RED FLAGS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STRUCTURING INDICATORS
□ Deposits just below ₹10 lakh CTR threshold (₹9-9.9 lakhs)
□ Multiple deposits on same day totaling above threshold
□ Customer breaks large cash into smaller deposits over days
□ Pattern of deposits in specific amounts (₹9,50,000, ₹9,99,000)
□ Coordinated deposits at multiple branches same day
□ Use of multiple individuals to make deposits (smurfing)
□ Sudden change in deposit patterns near month-end
□ Customer makes deposits at unusual times (late night)
□ Deposits clustered on specific days of week
□ Customer inquires about aggregation rules before depositing

CASH DEPOSIT PATTERNS
□ Cash deposits inconsistent with customer's stated business
□ Large cash deposits with no business rationale
□ Deposits in small denominations suggesting retail origin
□ Deposits in new, sequential notes suggesting organized source
□ Deposits in damaged or soiled notes
□ Cash deposits followed immediately by outgoing wire transfers
□ Customer unable to explain source of cash when asked
□ Deposits on behalf of third parties not related to business
□ Frequency of cash deposits unusual for business type
□ Cash activity increases without corresponding business growth

CASH WITHDRAWAL PATTERNS
□ Large cash withdrawals with no business explanation
□ Cash withdrawn immediately after deposits clear
□ Withdrawals in round numbers with no variation
□ Pattern of maximum daily ATM withdrawals
□ Withdrawals just before regulatory reporting deadlines
□ Customer requests specific denominations (all ₹500 notes)
□ Withdrawals claimed for payroll but inconsistent with employee count

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WIRE TRANSFER & ELECTRONIC PAYMENT RED FLAGS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

DOMESTIC WIRE TRANSFERS
□ Multiple wire transfers just below ₹10 lakh threshold
□ Frequent transfers between unrelated accounts
□ Wire transfers to/from multiple parties with no relationship
□ Transfers with vague or inconsistent purpose descriptions
□ Round number transfers suggesting non-commercial nature
□ Transfers immediately after cash deposits
□ Beneficiary changes at last minute
□ Transfer instructions overly complex or unusual
□ Same-day transfers in and out with no retention
□ Transfer patterns inconsistent with business type

INTERNATIONAL WIRE TRANSFERS
□ Transfers to/from high-risk or sanctioned countries
□ Transfers to offshore tax havens with no business rationale
□ Routing through multiple jurisdictions unnecessarily
□ Beneficiary in jurisdiction inconsistent with trade patterns
□ Transfer amount inconsistent with trade invoice values
□ Purpose vague ("family support", "gift", "loan")
□ Transfers split to avoid regulatory thresholds
□ No supporting trade documentation for large transfers
□ Customer insists on specific correspondent banks
□ Frequent amendments to transfer instructions
□ Transfers to shell companies or entities with no web presence
□ Currency exchange rates suggest hawala/hundi settlement

REMITTANCE PATTERNS
□ Multiple individuals remitting to same overseas beneficiary
□ Remittances inconsistent with customer's income level
□ Student remittances far exceeding educational expenses
□ Family remittances to countries with no family ties
□ Remittances to multiple countries with no explanation
□ LRS limit (USD 250K) utilized by entire family
□ Business purpose claimed but no supporting documentation
□ Frequent changes to beneficiary details

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ACCOUNT ACTIVITY PATTERN RED FLAGS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

TRANSACTION VELOCITY
□ Sudden spike in transaction volume (5x+ normal activity)
□ Account dormant for months then highly active
□ Transactions occurring at unusual hours
□ Multiple transactions on same day from different locations
□ Activity inconsistent with account opening stated purpose
□ Transactions clustered around specific dates/events
□ Frequency increases without business growth justification

FLOW-THROUGH PATTERNS
□ Credits totaling ₹50 lakhs+ within 5-10 days
□ 90%+ of credits withdrawn within 24-48 hours
□ Multiple incoming transfers immediately consolidated
□ Outgoing transfers to different beneficiaries than senders
□ Minimal balance retention (<5% of turnover)
□ Account used as pass-through rather than for storage
□ No legitimate business purpose for rapid movement

MULTIPLE CREDITOR PATTERNS
□ Receiving from 20+ unique sources in short period
□ Creditors have no apparent relationship to account holder
□ Creditors located across multiple states/countries
□ Incoming amounts relatively uniform (coordination indicator)
□ No invoices, contracts, or business documentation
□ Customer unable to explain relationships with creditors
□ Creditors are individuals but customer claims business income

ROUND NUMBER INDICATORS
□ 60%+ of transactions are round numbers (₹1L, ₹5L, ₹10L)
□ Transaction amounts show no decimal variations
□ Patterns suggest coordination rather than organic business
□ Round numbers inconsistent with typical retail/service pricing
□ Customer unable to explain why all amounts are round

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
BUSINESS-SPECIFIC RED FLAGS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CASH-INTENSIVE BUSINESSES
□ Restaurant revenues far exceeding seating capacity
□ Retail sales inconsistent with inventory purchases
□ No proportional increase in operating expenses despite revenue growth
□ Business shows profit but no tax filings
□ Cash deposits with no credit card/digital payment record
□ Business location doesn't support claimed revenue levels
□ Seasonal business showing uniform revenue year-round
□ No employee costs despite claiming large workforce

IMPORT/EXPORT BUSINESSES
□ Transaction amounts inconsistent with commodity market prices
□ Multiple payments for same shipment
□ Frequent Letter of Credit amendments
□ Goods description vague or frequently changing
□ Trading in commodities unrelated to business expertise
□ Shipments to/from high-risk jurisdictions
□ No customs or shipping documentation when requested
□ Over-invoicing or under-invoicing patterns
□ Triangular trade patterns with no economic rationale

PROFESSIONAL SERVICES
□ Consultant receiving payments from unrelated industries
□ Fee amounts inconsistent with professional norms
□ No retainer agreements or service contracts
□ Multiple clients all in high-risk businesses
□ Fees paid in cash for high-value services
□ Client relationships cannot be verified
□ Services claimed don't match professional qualifications

REAL ESTATE TRANSACTIONS
□ Property purchase with large cash down payment
□ Multiple buyers/sellers for single property
□ Purchase price significantly below/above market value
□ Quick flip sales (buy and resell within months)
□ Power of attorney used to obscure beneficial owner
□ Buyer has no apparent means to afford property
□ Seller cannot explain source of property acquisition funds
□ Transaction involves offshore entities

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
OCCUPATION-BASED RED FLAGS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STUDENTS
□ Receiving ₹10+ lakhs from multiple unknown sources
□ Income inconsistent with part-time work possibilities
□ Foreign remittances far exceeding tuition/living costs
□ Conducting business transactions despite student visa
□ Multiple students sharing same funding source
□ Remittances to countries other than home country

UNEMPLOYED/RETIRED
□ Significant deposits with no income source
□ Lifestyle expenses inconsistent with unemployment/pension
□ Conducting high-value transactions regularly
□ Receiving funds from business sources
□ Unable to explain source when questioned

SMALL BUSINESS OWNERS
□ Revenues 5-10x beyond reasonable business capacity
□ No corresponding inventory or supply purchases
□ Business expenses not proportional to claimed revenue
□ Cash intensity far beyond industry norms
□ Business activity inconsistent with stated nature
□ Multiple businesses in unrelated sectors

SALARIED EMPLOYEES
□ Credits exceeding annual salary by 3x+
□ Regular deposits beyond known employer salary
□ Business income claimed despite full-time employment
□ Foreign remittances with no documented reason
□ Lifestyle inconsistent with known salary level

POLITICALLY EXPOSED PERSONS (PEPs)
□ Wealth accumulation inconsistent with official salary
□ Use of family members' accounts to layer transactions
□ Shell companies with opaque ownership
□ Transactions with high-risk jurisdictions
□ Sudden wealth after assuming public office
□ Assets transferred to family before investigation
□ Transactions timed around procurement decisions

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
GEOGRAPHIC & JURISDICTIONAL RED FLAGS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

HIGH-RISK COUNTRIES (FATF)
□ Transactions to/from FATF blacklisted countries
□ Iran, North Korea, Myanmar (current blacklist)
□ Transactions with FATF grey list countries (check updated list)
□ Sanctioned jurisdictions per UN/US/EU lists

TAX HAVENS
□ Mauritius, Singapore, Cayman Islands, BVI (common tax havens)
□ Transfers to countries known for banking secrecy
□ Use of shell companies in offshore jurisdictions
□ No legitimate business purpose for offshore involvement

BORDER AREAS (INDIA-SPECIFIC)
□ Unusual activity involving border state customers
□ Transactions with Nepal, Bangladesh border areas
□ Cash movements toward border regions
□ Customers claiming business in border trade without documentation

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THIRD-PARTY & INTERMEDIARY RED FLAGS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

THIRD-PARTY TRANSACTIONS
□ Frequent third-party deposits into account
□ Third parties unrelated to customer or their business
□ Customer claims beneficial ownership lies elsewhere
□ Unable to identify ultimate beneficial owner
□ Third parties are PEPs or known high-risk individuals
□ Power of attorney used by multiple third parties

INTERMEDIARY USAGE
□ Lawyer/CA making deposits on behalf of client
□ Professional unable to identify ultimate client
□ Multiple clients all using same intermediary
□ Intermediary resists providing client information
□ Transactions structured through intermediary network

EMPLOYEE BEHAVIOR INDICATORS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

POTENTIAL INSIDER INVOLVEMENT
□ Employee processes transactions for same customer repeatedly
□ Employee bypasses approval processes
□ Employee accesses customer records without business need
□ Employee has unexplained relationship with customer
□ Employee discourages filing of STR/CTR
□ Employee lifestyle inconsistent with salary
□ Employee instructs customer on avoiding detection
□ Employee creates false records or backdates entries

TECHNOLOGY & DIGITAL PAYMENT RED FLAGS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

UPI/DIGITAL PAYMENTS
□ Multiple UPI accounts linked to single bank account
□ UPI transactions just below daily limits consistently
□ Merchant account receiving funds from numerous individuals
□ QR code payments from unrelated parties
□ Digital wallet loading/unloading patterns
□ Cryptocurrency purchase patterns
□ Gaming/gambling transactions concealing true purpose

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
DOCUMENTATION & RECORD RED FLAGS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

DOCUMENTATION ISSUES
□ Supporting documents appear forged or altered
□ Invoices don't match transaction amounts
□ Contracts lack specific terms or appear template
□ Dates on documents inconsistent with transaction timeline
□ Signatures vary across different documents
□ Documents provided only after multiple requests
□ Customer claims documents "lost" when requested

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ASSESSMENT & ACTION MATRIX
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SCORING GUIDE:
1 red flag = Heightened attention
2-3 red flags = Enhanced due diligence required
4-5 red flags = Senior review required
6+ red flags = Strong SAR consideration
10+ red flags = Immediate SAR filing + management escalation

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
□ Document all observations contemporaneously
□ Collect supporting evidence (transaction records, statements)
□ Review historical account activity
□ Check for related accounts (family, business)
□ Search internal watch lists and sanctions lists
□ Consult AML officer before proceeding
□ Prepare preliminary SAR if thresholds met
□ Do NOT alert customer to suspicions (tipping off violation)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
LEGAL REMINDERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

TIPPING OFF PROHIBITION:
Informing customer that SAR has been/will be filed is CRIMINAL OFFENSE
under PMLA Section 12. Penalties include imprisonment up to 2 years.

GOOD FAITH PROTECTION:
SAR filed in good faith provides legal protection even if subsequently
determined unfounded (PMLA Section 14).

FILING TIMELINE:
STR must be filed within 7 working days of classification as suspicious.
Late filing may result in penalties.
