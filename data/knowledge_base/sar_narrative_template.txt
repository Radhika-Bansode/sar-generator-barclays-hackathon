
SAR NARRATIVE STRUCTURE TEMPLATE
Financial Intelligence Unit - India (FIU-IND)
Suspicious Activity Report Writing Guidelines

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MANDATORY SECTIONS FOR SAR NARRATIVE

All Suspicious Activity Reports must contain the following six sections
in the order specified below:

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 1: BACKGROUND AND ACCOUNT INFORMATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Required Elements:
1. Customer Identification
   - Full legal name of account holder
   - Account number and account type (savings/current/NRE/NRO)
   - Date account was opened
   - Customer identification verified through (Aadhaar/PAN/Passport)

2. Customer Profile
   - Stated occupation and nature of business
   - Annual income as declared in KYC
   - Source of funds/wealth declaration
   - Residential address (complete with pin code)

3. Account History
   - Normal account behavior over past 6-12 months
   - Average monthly credit turnover (historical)
   - Average monthly debit turnover (historical)
   - Typical transaction patterns and frequencies
   - Nature of regular transactions (salary, business receipts, etc.)

4. KYC Compliance Status
   - Date of last KYC update
   - Risk category assigned (Low/Medium/High)
   - Customer Due Diligence measures applied
   - Enhanced Due Diligence status (if applicable)

Example Format:
"The account [ACCOUNT NUMBER] in the name of [CUSTOMER NAME] was opened on 
[DATE]. The customer identified themselves through [ID TYPE] and declared their 
occupation as [OCCUPATION] with annual income of ₹[AMOUNT]. Historical account 
activity shows monthly credit turnover averaging ₹[AMOUNT] from [SOURCE]."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 2: DESCRIPTION OF SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Chronological Narrative Required:
- Start with exact date suspicious activity commenced
- Describe each significant transaction with precision
- Include: Date, Amount, Source/Destination, Channel, Purpose
- Present facts in sequence without conclusory statements

Transaction Description Format:
"On [DATE], the account received a credit of ₹[EXACT AMOUNT] from 
[SOURCE ACCOUNT] belonging to [NAME] via [CHANNEL]. The transaction 
description stated: '[DESCRIPTION]'."

Key Requirements:
- State exact amounts (not "approximately" or "about")
- Use complete dates (DD-MM-YYYY format)
- Identify all parties involved (names, account numbers)
- Specify transaction channels (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS/Cash/Wire)
- Include transaction reference numbers when available

Behavioral Observations:
- Describe deviations from established account patterns
- Note timing patterns (clustering on specific dates)
- Identify unusual counter-party relationships
- Document inconsistencies with stated business purpose

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 3: SUSPICIOUS INDICATORS AND RED FLAGS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Regulatory Red Flags to Reference:

From FinCEN Guidelines:
- Structuring to avoid reporting thresholds (CTR avoidance)
- Rapid movement of funds (layering indicators)
- Transactions inconsistent with customer profile
- Use of multiple accounts for no apparent reason
- Unusual patterns of cash deposits followed by wire transfers

From FATF Typologies:
- Collection account patterns (multiple credits, single debit)
- Geographic inconsistencies (transactions from unusual locations)
- Round number transactions suggesting artificial structuring
- Cross-border transfers without clear economic purpose
- Use of intermediaries to obscure source/destination

From FIU-IND Circulars:
- Activity inconsistent with stated occupation/business
- Sudden surge in transaction volume
- Involvement of high-risk jurisdictions
- Attempts to maintain balances just below reporting thresholds
- Resistance to providing information during account review

Presentation Format:
"The following suspicious indicators were identified:
1. [RED FLAG NAME]: [SPECIFIC DETAILS AND EVIDENCE]
2. [RED FLAG NAME]: [SPECIFIC DETAILS AND EVIDENCE]"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 4: TRANSACTION PATTERN ANALYSIS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Quantitative Analysis Required:

Volume Analysis:
- Total credits during suspicious period: ₹[AMOUNT]
- Total debits during suspicious period: ₹[AMOUNT]
- Net change in balance: ₹[AMOUNT]
- Number of credit transactions: [COUNT]
- Number of debit transactions: [COUNT]

Source Analysis:
- Number of unique credit sources: [COUNT]
- Identification of sources (business relationships, individuals)
- Geographic distribution of sources
- Known relationships vs. unknown third parties

Temporal Analysis:
- Time period of suspicious activity: [START DATE] to [END DATE]
- Duration: [NUMBER] days
- Transaction frequency compared to historical norm
- Clustering patterns (if applicable)

Flow Pattern:
- Retention rate: [PERCENTAGE]% of funds retained
- Flow-through rate: [PERCENTAGE]% immediately transferred
- Destination of outgoing funds
- Purpose/beneficiary identification

Comparative Analysis:
"During the [TIME PERIOD], account activity increased by [X]x compared 
to the previous [TIME PERIOD] average of ₹[AMOUNT] monthly turnover."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 5: TYPOLOGY CLASSIFICATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Match Pattern to Known Money Laundering Typologies:

Common Typologies (FATF Reference):

1. PLACEMENT STAGE INDICATORS:
   - Multiple cash deposits below reporting threshold
   - Use of multiple accounts to fragment large amounts
   - Deposits followed immediately by wire transfers
   - Structuring to avoid Currency Transaction Reports

2. LAYERING STAGE INDICATORS:
   - Rapid movement between multiple accounts
   - Complex patterns obscuring audit trail
   - Use of intermediaries or shell entities
   - Cross-border transfers to multiple jurisdictions
   - Back-to-back transactions with no clear purpose

3. INTEGRATION STAGE INDICATORS:
   - Funds used for legitimate business transactions
   - Investment in real estate or securities
   - Purchase of high-value goods
   - Repayment of loans with suspicious fund sources

Specific Pattern Identification:
"The observed pattern is consistent with [TYPOLOGY NAME] as described in 
FATF Typology Report [YEAR]. Specifically, [DESCRIPTION OF MATCH]."

Example Typologies:
- Trade-Based Money Laundering (TBML)
- Smurfing/Structuring
- Cuckoo Smurfing
- Black Money Conversion
- Round-Tripping
- Layering through Multiple Accounts

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 6: CONCLUSION AND RECOMMENDATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Summary Statement:
Concisely restate the core suspicious activity without repetition.

Risk Assessment:
Based on [NUMBER] red flags and consistency with [TYPOLOGY], the 
activity presents [LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH] risk of money laundering.

Regulatory Compliance Statement:
"This report is filed in compliance with Section 12 of the Prevention 
of Money Laundering Act, 2002 (PMLA) and FIU-IND guidelines on reporting 
suspicious transactions."

Action Taken:
- Account flagged for enhanced monitoring: [YES/NO]
- Additional customer due diligence initiated: [YES/NO]
- Transaction restrictions applied: [YES/NO]
- Law enforcement notification: [IF APPLICABLE]

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WRITING STANDARDS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Language Requirements:
- Use clear, factual, objective language
- Avoid speculation or conclusory statements
- Do not use abbreviations without first defining them
- Write in active voice where possible
- Maintain professional, formal tone throughout

Prohibited Language:
- "We believe" or "We suspect" (state facts only)
- "Obviously" or "Clearly" (let facts speak)
- Emotional or judgmental terms
- Unsubstantiated claims

Required Precision:
- Always state exact amounts (₹5,00,000 not "approximately 5 lakhs")
- Use complete dates (15-01-2024 not "January")
- Cite specific regulatory references when applicable
- Include all transaction reference numbers

Length Guidelines:
- Typical SAR narrative: 800-1500 words
- Complex cases may require 2000-3000 words
- Focus on relevant facts; avoid unnecessary detail
- Each section should be substantive (minimum 100 words)
